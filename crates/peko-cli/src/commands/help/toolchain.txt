peko toolchain <SUBCOMMAND>

Inspects the installed build toolchains.

Toolchains are recorded in versions.json at the Peko root and live under
Compiler/toolchains. The layout differs by target: macos/<arch> and
linux/<arch> are per-architecture, ios splits into ios/arm/device,
ios/arm/simulator, and ios/x86_64, while android and windows are flat.
Each directory carries a toolchain.toml describing its target triple,
compile flags, includes, and the link step.

Toolchains are installed by `peko setup`, not by this command.

SUBCOMMANDS
    list                  load every installed toolchain's toolchain.toml and
                          report which parse and which fail

EXAMPLES
    peko toolchain list
