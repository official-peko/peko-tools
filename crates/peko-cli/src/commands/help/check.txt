peko check

Performs a deep checkup of the Peko toolchain installation. It confirms
the root exists, then recomputes a hash over the installed tree and
compares it to the one recorded at install time. Use this when something
seems wrong with your install.

The vendored subtrees (toolchains, llvm18, java, and the peko binary
itself) are excluded from the hash, so this verifies the installation
layout rather than the contents of every SDK.

OPTIONS
    --rehash              recompute and record the root hash, certifying
                          the installation as it currently stands. `peko
                          setup` runs this as its final step.
